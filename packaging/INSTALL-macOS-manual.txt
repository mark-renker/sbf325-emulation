Stereo Bucket Flanger - manual macOS install (if you prefer not to use the .pkg)

1. Copy "Stereo Bucket Flanger.component" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. Copy "Stereo Bucket Flanger.vst3" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Copy "Stereo Bucket Flanger.clap" to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
4. Because the bundles were downloaded (not installed by a .pkg), macOS quarantines them. Clear that once:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Stereo Bucket Flanger.component" \
                                     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Stereo Bucket Flanger.vst3" \
                                     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/"Stereo Bucket Flanger.clap"
5. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW. The plug-in appears under "Renky Denk".

"Stereo Bucket Flanger.app" is the standalone app; drag it to /Applications (clear quarantine the same way).
