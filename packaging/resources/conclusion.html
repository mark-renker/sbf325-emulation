Installed

Rescan plug-ins in your DAW (Logic: it scans on launch; Live: Preferences > Plug-Ins > Rescan; Reaper: Preferences > Plug-ins > Re-scan). Look under Renky Denk > Stereo Bucket Flanger.

The second input bus (“Ext CV”) takes a ±1 control signal from a DC-coupled interface when Ext CV is set to Sidechain; it replaces the LFO. To retune the model after measuring hardware, place a tunables.txt (one key=value per line, keys from src/dsp/Tunables.h) in ~/Library/Application Support/StereoBucketFlanger/; it is read each time the plug-in is loaded.
