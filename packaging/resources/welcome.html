Stereo Bucket Flanger @VERSION@

An open-source, non-commercial model of a two-channel analog bucket-brigade flanger of the late-1970s rack type, inspired by the Roland SBF‑325 Stereo Flanger: three flanger modes and a chorus mode, per-channel phase inversion, channel-B modulation inversion, calibrated feedback that never self-oscillates, NE570-style companding inside the delay loop, an external CV input, and a bucket-brigade delay simulated at its own variable clock. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roland Corporation.

On the next screen tick the formats you want (Audio Unit, VST3, CLAP, standalone app). Plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins; the app goes to /Applications. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW afterwards; it appears under Renky Denk > Stereo Bucket Flanger.

Requires macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.
